Technology

How pneumatic lithotripsy works

The Lithobuster uses compressed air to give a titanium bullet kinetic energy. The bullet strikes a stainless titanium probe that touches the stone, and the resulting high-vibration impacts fracture it into smaller pieces that are easier to remove or pass naturally.

  1. Compressed airAdjustable pressure drives the system.
  2. Titanium bulletAir accelerates the bullet inside the handpiece.
  3. Probe impactKinetic energy passes through the probe (0.8–2.0 mm).
  4. Stone fragmentationDirect contact breaks the stone into small fragments.

Which probe sizes are available?

Standard sizes are 0.8, 1.0, 1.2, 1.5, 1.8 and 2.0 mm, available for PCNL and URS applications.

Are the handpiece and probes compatible with the Swiss LithoClast?

Yes. The ZM2 pneumatic handpiece and the handpiece probes are listed as compatible with the Swiss LithoClast.

Which endoscopic systems does the light source work with?

It supports various endoscopic systems through universal light adapter compatibility (for example Olympus, Storz and Stryker).

What camera resolutions are offered?

Ultra HD 4K (3840 × 2160), Full HD (1920 × 1080) and HD (1280 × 720) endoscopic cameras are available.
